# 007 — Yearly OKRs by quarter, monthly board

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-17 · Supersedes [002](002-okr-cadence.md)

## Decision
- Users set **3–4 objectives per year**, each placed in a quarter, with **2–3 KRs** that each target one skill and a measurable result (certification, specific material, or N items).
- Plannable quarters: the rest of the current year plus next year. Finished years are archived.
- The board is **monthly** and always opens on the current month. Each month ends with a review.

## Why
A year is the natural horizon for career growth; quarters keep it actionable. Skill-linked, measurable KRs make growth objective. A quarterly board option was considered and dropped: one rhythm keeps planning simple.
