# 007 — Yearly OKRs by quarter, user-chosen board cycle

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-17 · Supersedes [002](002-okr-cadence.md)

## Decision
- Users set **3–4 objectives per year**, each placed in a quarter, with **2–3 KRs** that each target one skill and a measurable result (certification, specific material, or N items).
- Plannable quarters: the rest of the current year plus next year. Finished years are archived.
- The board runs in **monthly or quarterly cycles**, chosen at setup and changeable in Settings. Each cycle ends with a review.

## Why
A year is the natural horizon for career growth; quarters keep it actionable. Skill-linked, measurable KRs make growth objective. Cycle length varies by person, so it's a setting.
