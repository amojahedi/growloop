# 002 — Quarterly OKRs with monthly plans

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-17

## Context
Learning goals ("get better at roadmapping") take longer than a month, but a month is the right horizon for realistic planning and reflection.

## Decision
Objectives and key results are **quarterly**. Work is planned **per month**. At month end, a **rollover review** asks the user to carry over, backlog or drop unfinished items — nothing moves silently.

## Alternatives considered
- Monthly OKRs — too much ceremony; objectives would be rewritten every month.
- Open-ended objectives — loses the forcing function of a deadline.
- Auto carry-over — hides overcommitment, which is the main planning lesson users need.
